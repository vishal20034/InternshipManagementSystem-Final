INTERNSHIP ONBOARDING GUIDELINES & INSTRUCTIONS

@all — Please read carefully before proceeding
🌅 Good Morning / Afternoon / Evening — Please submit your attendance.
01📋 Attendance MANDATORY
  • Fill out the attendance form twice daily — morning & evening.
  • After submitting, share a screenshot in the group to ensure eligibility for your certificate.
  • 📎 Attendance Form: Click here to open attendance form
  • Missing daily attendance may lead to termination and no certificate.
  • Attendance is mandatory even if you have not yet received your Employee ID.
  • For your Employee ID: Find Employee ID here
  • If you don't receive your Employee ID, email hr@entrepreneurshipnetwork.net or join the support WhatsApp group.
02📁 Onboarding Document
  • 📄 Induction PDF: Open Induction PDF
  • 📋 All guidelines & instructions: Open Instructions Document
  • 🎬 Induction Session Video: Watch Induction Video (start from 10 minutes)
03💬 Communication Rules
  • Do not post complaints or spam in working groups.
  • For any issues: Open Support Group
  • Any misconduct or spamming may lead to termination.
  • For Employee ID issues: Employee ID Support Group
04📢 Daily Job Posting Task MANDATORY FOR ALL
  • Open Daily Job Posting Instructions
05📌 General Instructions
  • Check your group description for additional details.
  • Be patient — further instructions will be shared regularly.
06🧭 Discipline
  • Maintain professionalism in the group at all times.
  • Avoid unnecessary messages to prevent the group from becoming admin-only.
07⚠️ Important
  • Do not leave the group if you are confused. All next steps will be communicated there.
08🏆 Paid Projects & Positions for Best Performers
  • Open AI Development Playbook
  • Best performers get paid projects and paid positions.
  • Performance layers: entrepreneurshipnetwork.net/Layers
  • Email: hr@entrepreneurshipnetwork.net
